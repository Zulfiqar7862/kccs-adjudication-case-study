## Observed final-review correction — 2026-11-08

The AI-assisted submission had incorrectly asserted that the 64% closure share and 82% non-actionable share used different denominators. Reviewing brief sections 5–6 exposed the error: 78% applies within the non-actionable subset, while both 64% and 82% refer to all alerts. This review corrected the claim in this document and the risks register. The caution about unvalidated outcomes remains, but its original denominator argument was discarded. This is a record of an observed correction, not a reconstructed transcript or a claim of human review.
