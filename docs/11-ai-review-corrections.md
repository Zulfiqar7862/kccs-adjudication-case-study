# AI review and corrections

## AI assistance and review limits

AI assistance was used to inspect repository code and draft these documents. No external model API was used by the prototype during execution. The authorized case-study materials were consulted locally to create anonymized references; source files and identifying values were not copied into these public docs. No human review hours are claimed. The documented checks validate workflow mechanics, not clinical decisions; production qualification and accountable human review remain required.

## Corrections and guardrails captured in this submission

The inherited working log does not contain a dated transcript of specific AI mistakes. This document therefore does not invent one. The following are explicit review corrections/positions preserved in the submission; they should not be read as claims about a particular model output:

| Risky or unsupported interpretation | Correction retained here |
|---|---|
| The 70% no-human-review commitment is an appropriate optimization target. | Do not optimize it before validating its denominator, source quality, and false-closure risk; no automated disposition is in scope. |
| The source-pack rules-engine closure and non-actionable-alert percentages establish an automation success rate. | Their denominators differ and are not validated outcome labels; they cannot establish automated accuracy or savings. |
| Hash-chained audit exports prove seven-year immutable retention or guarantee one-hour retrieval. | A hash chain can detect some alteration; production retention, access, restore, and retrieval objectives need qualified controls. |
| A reminder attempt or manually recorded receipt proves a regulator was notified. | Reminders are internal only; human-attested receipts are not independently verified delivery. Regulatory obligations remain independent of case status and reopen events. |
| A 90-day milestone means all production controls and 34-customer rollout will be complete. | Use day 90 as a gated decision for one bounded pilot; wider onboarding remains individually conditional. |

The traceable requirements, assumptions, and counterarguments are in [where we land](01-where-we-land.md) and [risks/open questions](09-risks-assumptions-and-open-questions.md).
